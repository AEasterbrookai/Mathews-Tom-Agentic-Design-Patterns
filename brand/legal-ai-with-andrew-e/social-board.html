Measured on the 26 designs on this board, each counted equally. Photo slots are excluded. Every pixel is split between the two palette colours it blends, so antialiased edges count fairly. Rendered in a headless browser at 1360 px wide, 20 November 2026. Red marks only the [E] brackets, and the thinner yellow lines on black cards make yellow a trace too. Weighted by pixel area instead, the large banners dominate: paper 92.5%, ink 6.4%, green 1.0%, yellow 0.02%, red 0.03%. Real photographs will add their own colour and are not part of these figures.

Colour rules
[1]Paper and ink do the work. Most designs are white with ink type.
[2]Red is only for the [E] brackets. Headlines stay in ink.
[3]Green is only for series tags, underline strokes, vertical rules and the numbers [1] [2] [3].
[4]Yellow is only for the series tag and underline stroke on black cards. It never appears on white.
[5]One accent colour per design. Where red, green or yellow is used, the other two are not. A design with the red [E] mark has no green or yellow; a design with a green tag has an ink [E].
[6]About one black card in every thirteen designs. Real photographs only.
